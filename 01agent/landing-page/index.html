Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about 01Agent

How is 01Agent 60-80% faster than other automation tools?

+

01Agent uses intelligent task routing to automatically choose the fastest execution method for each task. Instead of relying on slow GUI automation, it prioritizes CLI commands, background scripts, and optimized UI detection with smart caching.

What platforms does 01Agent support?

+

01Agent works on Windows 10/11, macOS 10.14+, and Linux (Ubuntu 18.04+). It includes full support for PowerShell, CMD, Bash, and cross-platform GUI automation.

Is the 3-month free trial really free?

+

Yes! The 3-month free trial includes all premium features with no limitations. No credit card required to start. After the trial, you can choose to continue with our affordable Pro plan or use the basic free version.

How secure is 01Agent?

+

Security is our top priority. 01Agent runs locally on your machine, uses encrypted communication, and never sends sensitive data to external servers. All automation scripts are sandboxed and require explicit user permission.

Can I customize 01Agent for my specific workflow?

+

Absolutely! 01Agent includes advanced settings for execution strategies, performance tuning, and custom integrations. Enterprise users get additional customization options and dedicated support.

What kind of support do you offer?

+

We offer comprehensive documentation, video tutorials, community forums, and email support. Pro users get priority support, and Enterprise customers receive dedicated support with custom SLAs.
